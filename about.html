About

AI football predictions, published before kick-off.

AIGoalie is a football prediction platform that uses historical match data, team form, scoring patterns, odds, and availability signals to publish daily model outputs.

What AIGoalie does

Every day, AIGoalie scans upcoming football fixtures and ranks matches by the model's confidence. The public pages show the predicted side, win confidence, total-goals signal, match status, and a simple explanation of the model's view.

What Pro adds

Pro unlocks the extended prediction window and deeper model detail, including exact expected-goals estimates, both-teams-to-score estimates, and opponent-isolated strength signals. These are model outputs, not guarantees.

How to read the confidence score

The displayed win confidence is the model's estimated strength of the selected side in that matchup. It should be read as a ranking and decision-support signal, not as certainty. Football has draws, red cards, injuries, rotation, and randomness.

Track record

AIGoalie keeps a public track record of graded predictions. Predictions are published before kick-off and graded after full-time, so users can judge the model against historical results rather than marketing claims.
