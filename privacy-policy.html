This Privacy Policy explains how AIGoalie Ltd (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects and uses information when you visit or use https://ai-goalie.com and related services.

AIGoalie Ltd, Unit 82a, James Carter Road, Mildenhall, IP28 7DE, United Kingdom, is the Data Controller for personal data processed under this policy.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email and authentication credentials if you create an account (managed via Google Firebase Authentication).

  • Messages you send us: e.g. the name, email and content you submit through our contact form.
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and device information for analytics and security.

3. Legal Bases (UK/EU GDPR)

We process data only where lawful, including: performance of a contract, legitimate interests (e.g. service improvement, fraud prevention), consent (for non-essential cookies, where required), or compliance with legal obligations.

4. Advertising

AIGoalie does not display third-party advertising or load advertising network scripts.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential storage for account access, preferences and security. We use Umami and Google Analytics to understand site usage. You can manage or delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings.

6. Data Sharing

We share data only with trusted providers, including Stripe (payments), Google Firebase (authentication), Umami and Google Analytics (analytics), and essential hosting/content-delivery providers. We do not sell personal data for money.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary for the purposes described or as required by law.

AIGoalie assistant chats

When you use the assistant, we store your questions, answers, relevant match context and diagnostic information for up to 90 days to provide chat history, support and service improvements. An authorized site administrator may review this information. Your question, recent conversation context and relevant football data are sent to Anthropic (Claude) to generate the response. Do not include sensitive personal information in chat.

You can delete a conversation from History. This removes its chat content from our live service, including the administrator's transcript view. Limited usage records, such as token counts, cost, account identifier and request times, are retained for up to 12 months for service accounting and abuse prevention. Backups expire within 30 days; deleted content may remain in those backups until they expire. We retain aggregate spending totals without chat content to enforce service budgets.

Short-lived request counters help prevent abuse. Verified email is required for customer access; free-user security checks use Cloudflare Turnstile when enabled. The browser stores a conversation identifier to resume your chat, not the transcript itself. Contact aigoalie.mail@gmail.com for access or deletion requests beyond individual chats.
