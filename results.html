How grading works

A pick counts as won only when the model's selected team won the match; draws are reported separately and count against the win rate, not for it. The “top call” is the single highest-confidence prediction of each day. Every graded prediction remains browsable on its original day page — pick any past date from the predictions board and the results are stamped onto the same rows that showed the pre-match call.
